Santa Clara University · HCI Capstone

Click Cleverly: Design Walkthrough

Main ideas: (1) an interactive consent quiz that turns legal clauses into plain questions, and (2) a color-coded risk scale that flags concerning clauses within the contract.

Read the full write-up on Medium ↗

Where it started

The earliest sketch tested a click-to-reveal popup on a live Terms of Service page: user-initiated & not intrusive.

Feature 1 = Interactive Consent Quiz

Legal clauses become direct yes/no questions — "Do you agree to let this company sell your data?" — so users engage instead of skimming past them.

Feature 2 = Color-Coded Risk Highlighting

The actual TOS text is overwritten by color-coded highlights and plain-language explanations to highlight risky clauses.

Feature 3 = Personalized Preferences

Users can choose and specify what is most important to them in privacy settings
